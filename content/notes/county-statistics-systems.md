County planning depends on many kinds of information: population, service coverage, public finance, infrastructure, livelihoods, programme performance, and citizen priorities.

Too often, that information is scattered across departments, reports, spreadsheets, and systems that do not speak to each other.

A county statistics system should help organize indicators, metadata, codelists, data sources, reporting cycles, and dashboards into a coherent whole.

The goal is not to create another document. The goal is to make public decisions easier to understand, track, and improve.
